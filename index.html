Two ways to put a new avatar on your Replay Poker profile — straight from this Vault (the easy way), or from a friend’s profile. Both finish in the same Replay upload box.

🖼
Method 1 — Get It From the Avatar VaultRecommended · Four ways to grab it
Part A — Open Replay’s upload box
  1. Keep this Vault open and open Replay Poker in a second browser tab (or window side by side).
  2. On Replay, click the drop-down arrow at the top right and choose “Profile”.
  3. When your profile opens, click “Edit Profile”.
  4. Click the “Change” button next to your current avatar. The upload box opens — it says “Select Files to Upload · or Drag and Drop, Copy and Paste Files.”
Part B — Grab your avatar from the Vault (pick one)

🖱 Drag & Drop Fastest

Find your avatar in the Avatar Gallery tab. Click and hold on the picture, drag it over to the Replay tab/window, and let go inside the upload box.

Tip: put the two windows side by side, or drag onto the Replay tab at the top, wait for it to open, then drop in the box.

📋 Copy & Paste

Click Copy under your avatar (or right-click the picture → “Copy Image”). Go to the Replay upload box and press Ctrl + V (Mac: ⌘ + V).

🔗 Copy the Link

Click Link under your avatar to copy its web address. In the Replay upload box, click the chain-link icon in the left-hand menu, paste the link with Ctrl + V, and click the arrow / submit button.

💾 Download to Your Computer

Click Save under your avatar — it goes to your Downloads folder. In the Replay upload box click “Select Files to Upload”, open Downloads, and double-click the avatar file.

Handy for keeping your own copy, or for using it on another site later.

Part C — Finish up
  1. Your avatar appears in the box. Click the blue “Upload” button in the lower right corner.
  2. In the next window, click the red “Update Profile” button — you’re done! 🎉
If a paste or drop doesn’t take, click once inside the upload box first and try again, or use the Save option — it works on every browser.
👥
Method 2 — Copy It From a Friend’s ProfileThe original way · 11 steps
Copy your friend’s avatar
  1. On Replay, click the drop-down arrow at the top right and choose “Friends”.
  2. Click the name of the friend who has the avatar you want.
  3. Click on your friend’s avatar.
  4. The avatar pops up as a larger picture.
  5. Right-click on the opened picture and choose “Copy Image”.
Put it on your profile
  1. Click the drop-down arrow again and choose “Profile”.
  2. When your profile opens, click “Edit Profile”.
  3. Click the “Change” button next to your avatar.
  4. When the upload box appears, press Ctrl + V together to paste the picture into the box.
  5. The picture appears in the box — click the blue “Upload” button in the lower right corner.
  6. In the next window, click the red “Update Profile” button — you’re done!
